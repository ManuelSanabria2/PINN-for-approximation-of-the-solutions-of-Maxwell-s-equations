# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, researchers, and engineers interested in Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN) and electromagnetism.

## Product Purpose
Interactive simulation of Maxwell's Equations using a PINN. Allows real-time evaluation of electromagnetic fields ($E_z$, $B_x$, $B_y$), relative permittivity ($\varepsilon_r$), relative permeability ($\mu_r$), and boundary/initial conditions in a 2D PEC cavity.

## Brand Personality
Scientific, ultra-premium, dark mode, precise, analytical, educational.

## Anti-references
Simple SaaS templates, flat gray designs, low-contrast scientific text, over-rounded toy-like inputs.

## Design Principles
1. **Scientific Integrity**: Visualizations must represent physical fields accurately and feel research-grade.
2. **Immersive Interaction**: Enable fluid controls and dynamic adjustments to reinforce the physics behind the equations.
3. **High contrast & Legibility**: Ensure all mathematical formulations and metrics are highly legible against the dark background.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web contrast ratios, responsive scaling, respect reduced-motion settings.
